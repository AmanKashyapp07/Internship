# Internship Journey

> **Rejections are not the end. They are part of the process.**

Arcesium. MotorQ. Salesforce.
Three rejections, but **zero reasons to give up**.

Every rejection is feedback. Every failure is experience. Every attempt brings me one step closer to the opportunity I am looking for.

Let people celebrate their win (sometimes just a lottery). Mute all the noise. Keep your head down and focus on your goal.

I don't need to succeed every time. I just need to **keep improving, keep applying, and keep trying**.

One day, I'll look back at these rejections and realize they were just part of the journey.

**Keep going. Keep learning. Keep building. Don't stop until you make it.**
