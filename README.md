## The Path of Perseverance

Every great outcome is preceded by a long stretch of unglamorous effort that nobody sees. The applications that go nowhere, the interviews that don't convert, the silence after a rejection email, none of that is proof of where I'll end up. It's just the cost of trying.

My internship search is a campaign, not a verdict. Each rejection isn't a statement on my worth. It's data. It tells me something to refine, sharpen, or try differently next time. The offer I eventually get won't erase the rejections that came before it, it'll be built on them.

---

### The Hardest Part

The hardest part isn't the rejections themselves, it's watching SOME (who?) of my batchmates already living the life I'm still chasing: happy, settled, celebrating, while I'm still standing at the starting line. We all know how some bunch of people got their internship offers (can't say more, system will retaliate). That gap between where they are and where I am feels like its own quiet kind of torture. I hope I get one soon, because it's getting tougher each day, I can't see these guys happy, it's the greatest pain one could ever experience.
